Past Projects

2025–2026 Cohort

Completed
Philadelphia Zoo

Climate Impacts on Zoo Attendance

Quantified how shifting weather patterns and climate change have affected seasonal attendance at the Philadelphia Zoo, and developed a forecasting model to help the Zoo anticipate attendance trends and adapt its operations and business planning accordingly.

Completed
FMC Corporation

Understanding Climate Impacts on Growers

Assessed climate-related risks — including drought, flooding, pest pressure, and temperature shifts — facing farmers in key FMC markets, and identified adaptation strategies. The project also evaluated how FMC's portfolio of products can support growers in responding to changing climate conditions.

Completed
PA Fibershed

Textile Waste Management in Philadelphia

Assessed the scale, impact, and potential of textile waste management in the Philadelphia region. The project examined existing models locally and abroad, mapped donation centers and shelters, and developed an actionable roadmap for a regional textile recycling or downcycling facility.

Completed
Penn School of Veterinary Medicine

Penn Vet Climate & Sustainability Action Plan

Supported Penn Vet in developing its first Climate and Sustainability Action Plan (CSAP 1.0), aligned with Penn's university-wide CSAP 4.0 — making Penn Vet the first Penn school to create a school-specific CSAP. Included a baseline assessment, gap analysis, and preliminary recommendations.

Completed
The Water Center at Penn & Global Water Alliance

IJWEJ Special Edition: Water Equity & Justice

Curated and published a special edition of the International Journal for Water Equity and Justice (IJWEJ), an open-access, peer-reviewed journal dedicated to advancing water equity globally. Fellows served in editorial, communications, and project management roles.

2024–2025 Cohort

Partner Project Summary
Philly TREEs Urban Greenspace Equity Analyzed how Philadelphia's affordable housing exemption impacted street tree planting and greenspace equity, producing a report on city code and community perspectives.
The Water Center at Penn Water Reuse Policy Investigated water reuse opportunities and challenges in the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic, assessing policy frameworks, economic impacts, and public perceptions.
Sally Blagg Family Foundation Outdoors for All: Nature Access & Equity Worked to understand and reduce physical and psycho-emotional barriers preventing equal access to green spaces in Philadelphia, through community interviews, a conference, and a community toolkit and storymap.
Penn Kleinman Center for Energy Policy Agriculture, Energy & Land Use Conflict Delivered a report on strategies to identify and manage land-use conflict between renewable energy and farmland, with agrivoltaics as a potential solution.
