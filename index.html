Investing in the Future of our Planet

Climate Leaders at Penn is a fellowship program driven by students, guided by faculty, and supported by the University. Founded in 2020, we aim to accelerate, nurture, and ignite climate-focused entrepreneurship, leadership, and policy initiatives across Penn's graduate schools.

Our program brings together graduate students from across the university — from Wharton to Weitzman, from Engineering to the Law School — united by a shared commitment to addressing the climate crisis.

What We Do

Climate Leaders at Penn offers fellows three core experiences throughout the year.

  • Community & Networking

    Fellows become part of a tight-knit cohort of climate-minded graduate students from across Penn's schools and disciplines. Through happy hours, social events, and structured networking activities, we build the cross-disciplinary relationships that define a career in climate.

  • Masterclasses

    At least once each semester, fellows gain exclusive access to masterclasses led by leading voices from inside and outside the University — spanning climate tech, policy, finance, and sustainability. These intimate sessions offer direct engagement with the experts shaping the climate landscape.

  • Capstone Projects

    Fellows collaborate in interdisciplinary teams with real-world partners from industry, government, and the nonprofit sector. Each team produces a substantive, professionally relevant deliverable — tackling live climate challenges and building a portfolio of meaningful impact.
